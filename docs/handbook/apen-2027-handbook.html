APEN AI, Coding & Robotics
Competition Handbook

APEN 2027 · Lagos (Hybrid) · Organized by the Association of Private Educators in Nigeria (APEN), in partnership with Imperial EdTech

Theme: “Smart Solutions for Tomorrow’s Nigeria — Design. Build. Intelligize.”

1. Welcome

Welcome to the APEN 2027 AI, Coding & Robotics Competition. This year sharpens the focus: each age division owns one real-world Nigerian challenge, solved with hardware engineering and generative AI.

  • Primary Schools tackle Agriculture
  • Secondary Schools tackle Power
  • Sixth Form Colleges tackle Security

The competition pushes students to innovate at the intersection of physical computing and AI. The 2027 edition introduces a 4-stage Innovation Funnel that takes teams from pure design thinking, through a standardized hardware build, into open-ended AI integration (“Intelligize”), and culminates in a live Grand Finale Battle. Every team receives the same base kit; the AI layer is left open to their creativity — fair judging, genuine innovation rewarded.

2. Competition Overview

All projects follow a structured Engineering Design Thinking framework:

  1. Empathize — Conduct field research. Identify real pain points in Nigerian farms, homes, or communities.
  2. Define — Frame the challenge from that research. Set measurable goals.
  3. Ideate — Sketch design concepts. Select the most feasible hardware and AI approach.
  4. Prototype — Assemble the standardized APEN kit. Run real-world tests.
  5. Improve — Integrate the AI layer. Fix flaws found in testing. Optimize the experience.
  6. Communicate — Present the solution, its impact, and what the AI layer adds.

3. AI in the Competition

Generative AI & Vibe Coding

Coding is evolving. Foundational logic still matters, but students now use AI to accelerate development:

  • Prompt Engineering — directing large language models to interpret sensor data, generate alerts, or write logic blocks.
  • Vibe Coding — building dashboards and data-logging interfaces by describing intent to an AI rather than writing syntax from scratch.
  • Edge AI & Computer Vision — advanced teams may run lightweight ML models directly on microcontrollers to recognize patterns or anomalies.

Modalities

  • Physical Hands-on — direct work with the APEN kit and add-ons.
  • Virtual Online — remote AI integration, dashboard building, digital submissions.
  • Hybrid Learning — physical builds combined with virtual AI mentorship.

4. Equipment & Resources

Each division uses one common kit tailored to its niche, so every team in a division builds the same core project and judging stays fair and consistent. An Innovation Store is available to all participating schools — because Stage 3 is open-ended, teams may purchase add-on component packs (extra sensors, camera modules, connectivity boards, power add-ons) to push their AI layer further.

Safety note (Secondary — Power Division): all power-sensing activities use a low-voltage DC proxy circuit to represent grid power. No team wires directly into live 220V household current.

6. Stages of the Competition

Stage 1 — Design (the problem-finding sprint). Pure design thinking and empathy. Conduct local field research, interview users, identify real pain points. Deliverable: 3-minute video pitch by January 29, 2027. Every team continues to Stage 2; the Design score counts toward each team’s overall total.

Stage 2 — Build (the standardized hardware phase). Closed-ended technical execution and fairness. Assemble and program the official APEN division kit so core hardware logic functions reliably. Deliverable: functional prototype by February 21, 2027. Every team uses identical hardware, so judging is consistent across all schools. Build submissions are judged during the mid-term break, and the top teams in each division advance to Stage 3.

Stage 3 — Intelligize (the open AI innovation phase). Open-ended AI integration and creativity. Teams integrate generative AI, machine learning, vibe coding, or edge AI to turn raw hardware data into human insight, drawing on the Innovation Store for any add-ons their vision requires. Deliverable: final AI prompt log, project video and design poster by March 14, 2027. Judges review these submissions from March 15 to 18.

Stage 4 — Battle (the Grand Finale). On March 23, 2027, every team that completes Stage 3 presents a 3–5 minute final pitch covering hardware functionality, AI layer integration, and real-world impact, live in Lagos (virtual and/or physical). After the presentations, the judges deliver their final verdict and the winners are crowned APEN 2027 Champions.

11. How to Participate

Step 1 — Register & pitch. The school’s ICT or STEM teacher registers for their division and completes kit payment by January 19, 2027. Join the official announcements channel. The student team records and uploads the Stage 1 video pitch by January 29.

Step 2 — Receive & build. Receive the official APEN kit by January 25. Join live AI and coding mentorship sessions. Assemble and test the standardized hardware from February 1 to 21, ready for judging.

Step 3 — Intelligize & innovate. During Stage 3 (March 1–14), integrate the chosen AI, ML, or vibe-coded layer, drawing on the Innovation Store for any add-ons. Upload the final video, design poster, and AI prompt log by March 14.

Step 4 — Grand Finale & recognition. Every team that completes Stage 3 presents live in Lagos (virtual and/or physical) on March 23, 2027, taking part in the presentations and Q&A, followed by the judges’ final verdict and the award ceremony. Champions are crowned and receive prizes and mentorship.

12. Participant Agreement

All participants agree to:

  • Use only approved, official APEN division kit components for the Stage 2 standardized build
  • Submit original work and document all AI prompt engineering in the prompt log
  • Follow the Engineering Design Thinking process throughout
  • Meet all submission and presentation deadlines across all seven phases
  • Take part in required presentations — Stage 1 video pitch, final video pitch, Grand Finale Battle
  • Comply with safety rules: Secondary Power Division teams use only low-voltage DC proxy circuits; no direct 220V mains wiring, ever
  • Attend the live Grand Finale Battle on March 23, 2027 after completing Stage 3, or forfeit the team’s place
  • Respect the decisions of the judging panel and industry experts

13. Conclusion

APEN 2027 integrates engineering design thinking with hands-on tech skills, empowering students to solve real Nigerian challenges in agriculture, power, and security. By following a structured problem-solving approach and incorporating AI, participants develop innovative, user-focused solutions for tomorrow’s Nigeria.

The Grand Finale Battle on March 23, 2027 showcases the best of these innovations and crowns the APEN 2027 Champions — the result of an eight-week challenge spanning four stages: Design, Build, Intelligize, and Battle.
